# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Primary: recruiters and talent teams screening for hybrid CAE + ML / simulation-automation roles. They skim fast for impact, keywords and proof, often on a phone, before forwarding a profile to an engineering lead. Secondary: engineering leads who open the project detail to judge depth.

## Product Purpose
Personal recruiter portfolio for Alfred Chettiar, Lead CAE Durability & Simulation Automation Engineer (3.5+ years R&D at Royal Enfield). Success: a visitor understands within 30 seconds who he is, what he has delivered, and how to contact him or download the resume.

## Positioning
A physics-literate ML engineer: his edge is understanding the structural mechanics (durability, fatigue, FE) behind the models he builds, backed by industry results and a public, benchmarked surrogate-optimization project.

## Operating Context
Visitors arrive from LinkedIn, email or a resume link (OpenGraph preview card exists). They scan the hero, impact metrics, project cards (filterable by domain), the P-01 "Architecture & Pipeline" detail with verified metrics and four key plots, publications and education, then contact.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Static HTML5, CSS3 and vanilla JS; no build step; deploys from `main` via GitHub Pages.
- Must stay fast and mobile responsive.
- Redesign scope (Nov 2026): design only. Keep every fact and sentence as is; change layout, color, type and motion.
- Owner chose a light theme (binding; supersedes the earlier dark-theme rule).

## Evidence on Hand
- Impact metrics and publications from SPEC.md.
- P-01 results verified against the structural-surrogate-optimizer repo (`models/*.json`); four plots in `assets/p1/`.
- Resume PDF: `assets/Alfred_Resume.pdf`. OG image: `assets/og-image.png`.
- No testimonials, logos or employer artwork; do not fabricate any. Enterprise work is proprietary and described at a high level only.
- Open content issue: both 2023 journal entries show the same title (SPEC.md gives one topic for two journals).

## Product Principles
1. Every number shown must be traceable to a source; no inflated or unverified claims.
2. Lead with physics understanding, prove it with ML results.
3. Scannable in 30 seconds on a phone; depth on demand.
4. Honest limitations stay visible next to results.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG 2.1 AA contrast, keyboard access, visible focus, 44px touch targets, and full prefers-reduced-motion support.
